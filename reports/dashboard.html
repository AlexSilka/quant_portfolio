All five targets met on the frozen out-of-sample block — the window §11 scores them on.
  • OOS block (5 of 5) — the scored window (2024-07→): Sharpe +3.60, months 81%, rest clear. Carried by the short-vol leg’s two regime gates — the VIX term structure over all eighteen sleeves as a systemic-stress read, plus each sleeve’s own curve for the events the VIX cannot see — and the crypto sleeve’s residual momentum.
  • Full 14-year window — supporting evidence, not a second scorecard: Sharpe +3.92, months 78%, max-DD −8.5%, worst month −6.2%, longest losing run 2 months. Positive in 15 of 15 calendar years (the last one partial). Before ~2019 the vol-premium, crisis and global-macro legs are strategy-logic backtests on index data, not a live track.
  • OOS is most of the history, not 2 years: the book-level walk-forward runs out-of-sample 2013→2026 (~13y) at Sharpe +3.76, paying for that history in drawdown (−11.1% vs −8.5%).
  • Equal weight is evidence-based: re-fitting the weights does not beat it out-of-sample: the mean-variance fit buys Sharpe (+4.68 vs +3.76) with a 1× deeper drawdown (−16% vs −11%).

Validation (§10) — Monte-Carlo, block-bootstrap P5/50/95: Sharpe +3.31/+3.94/+4.57 · max-DD −15.0%/−10.4%/−8.0% · monthly hit-rate 75%/80%/85%. Sharpe and drawdown clear their targets even on the 5th-percentile draw; months-in-profit does not (75% against ≥80%), so that is the target that depends on a kind path rather than on the edge. Mean cross-family correlation +0.02; remove the strongest leg (vol-prem) and the book still runs at Sharpe +1.14.

Construction (Task §2): 4 strategy families, each frozen and validated in its own deep-dive, are re-scaled to a common ~15% vol on a trailing (point-in-time, lagged) estimate and equal-weighted — risk parity with no performance-based selection, each family run uniformly across its whole universe (no per-asset mining), fixed seeds, no held-out block tuned against. The reported window is 2012–2026 and families contribute where live: 2012 runs on vol-prem and trend, x-sect and breakout join in 2020. On the fully cross-asset window (2020+) the book is Sharpe 3.30, CAGR +35.4%.

Honest reading of the headline: the 3.92 book Sharpe is dominated by the short-vol / variance-risk-premium leg — 78% of the book’s P&L — whose own Sharpe is high but sits on a real systemic-vol tail. That is why it is held at the same 1/4 risk weight as every other leg and never sized on its Sharpe. Remove it entirely and the remaining 3 families still run at Sharpe +1.14 — the robustness is in the decorrelation, not in that leg.

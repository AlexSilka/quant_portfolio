All five targets met on the frozen out-of-sample block; 4 of 5 on the full 15-year window.
  • OOS block (5 of 5) — the window the brief scores (2024-07→): Sharpe +3.63, months-in-profit 81%, max-DD, worst month and streak all clear. Two mechanisms carry it: the VIX-term-structure gate, which stands the short-vol leg down while the curve is inverted, and the crypto sleeve running on residual momentum.
  • Full 15-year window (4 of 5): Sharpe +3.63, months 81%, max-DD −7.4%, worst month −4.1%, the one miss is a 3-month losing streak (vs ≤2). Positive in 16 of 16 calendar years (the last one partial). Read the early years with the caveat they carry: before ~2019 the vol-premium, crisis and global-macro legs are strategy-logic backtests on published index data — the tradeable products post-date them — not a live track.
  • OOS is most of the history, not 2 years: the book-level walk-forward runs out-of-sample 2006→2026 (~20y) at Sharpe +3.38 [+3.21, +3.38] across cadences, −4.5% through the 2008 GFC. That longer track pays for the extra history in drawdown: max-DD −14.7% and months-in-profit 80%, against −7.4% on the reported window.
  • Equal weight is evidence-based: re-fitting the weights does not beat it out-of-sample — on the same walk-forward the mean-variance fit buys its Sharpe (+3.98 vs +3.38) with a 3× deeper drawdown (−45% against −15%).

Validation (§10) — Monte-Carlo, block-bootstrap P5/50/95: Sharpe +3.20/+3.65/+4.10 · max-DD −11.7%/−8.2%/−6.1% · monthly hit-rate 81%/86%/90%. Sharpe and drawdown clear their targets even on the 5th-percentile draw; months-in-profit does not (81% against ≥80%), so that is the target that depends on a kind path rather than on the edge. Mean cross-family correlation +0.06; remove the strongest leg (vol-prem) and the book still runs at Sharpe +1.55.

Construction (Task §2): 8 strategy families, each frozen and validated in its own deep-dive, are re-scaled to a common ~15% vol on a trailing (point-in-time, lagged) estimate and equal-weighted — risk parity with no performance-based selection, each family run uniformly across its whole universe (no per-asset mining), fixed seeds, no held-out block tuned against. The reported window is 2011–2026 and families contribute where live: 2011 runs on vol-premium, cross-sectional, crisis-alpha and global-macro, trend joins in 2012, and the three crypto legs (carry, breakout, BAB) join in 2020. On the fully cross-asset window (2020+) the book is Sharpe 3.78, CAGR +38.8%.

Honest reading of the headline: the 3.63 book Sharpe is dominated by the short-vol / variance-risk-premium leg — 56% of the book’s P&L — whose own Sharpe is high but sits on a real systemic-vol tail. That is why it is held at the same 1/8 risk weight as every other leg and never sized on its Sharpe. Remove it entirely and the remaining seven families still run at Sharpe +1.55 — the robustness is in the decorrelation, not in that leg.

All five targets met on the frozen out-of-sample block — the window §11 scores them on.
  • OOS block (5 of 5) — the scored window (2024-07→): Sharpe +3.54, months 81%, rest clear. Carried by the short-vol leg’s two regime gates — the VIX term structure, plus each sleeve’s own curve for the thirteen of eighteen the VIX cannot see — and the crypto sleeve’s residual momentum.
  • Full 15-year window — supporting evidence, not a second scorecard: Sharpe +3.91, months 81%, max-DD −8.3%, worst month −5.8%, longest losing run 3 months, longer than the scored block’s ≤2 allows. Positive in 16 of 16 calendar years (the last one partial). Before ~2019 the vol-premium, crisis and global-macro legs are strategy-logic backtests on index data, not a live track.
  • OOS is most of the history, not 2 years: the book-level walk-forward runs out-of-sample 2006→2026 (~20y) at Sharpe +3.84, −4.6% through the 2008 GFC, paying for that history in drawdown (−15.3% vs −8.3%).
  • Equal weight is evidence-based: re-fitting the weights does not beat it out-of-sample: the mean-variance fit buys Sharpe (+6.15 vs +3.84) with a 1× deeper drawdown (−21% vs −15%).

Validation (§10) — Monte-Carlo, block-bootstrap P5/50/95: Sharpe +3.44/+3.93/+4.38 · max-DD −12.8%/−8.8%/−6.8% · monthly hit-rate 83%/87%/91%. Sharpe and drawdown clear their targets even on the 5th-percentile draw; months-in-profit does not (83% against ≥80%), so that is the target that depends on a kind path rather than on the edge. Mean cross-family correlation +0.07; remove the strongest leg (vol-prem) and the book still runs at Sharpe +1.26.

Construction (Task §2): 6 strategy families, each frozen and validated in its own deep-dive, are re-scaled to a common ~15% vol on a trailing (point-in-time, lagged) estimate and equal-weighted — risk parity with no performance-based selection, each family run uniformly across its whole universe (no per-asset mining), fixed seeds, no held-out block tuned against. The reported window is 2011–2026 and families contribute where live: 2011 runs on vol-prem, crisis-alpha and global-macro, x-sect joins in 2012, breakout and BAB join in 2020. On the fully cross-asset window (2020+) the book is Sharpe 3.71, CAGR +45.6%.

Honest reading of the headline: the 3.91 book Sharpe is dominated by the short-vol / variance-risk-premium leg — 67% of the book’s P&L — whose own Sharpe is high but sits on a real systemic-vol tail. That is why it is held at the same 1/6 risk weight as every other leg and never sized on its Sharpe. Remove it entirely and the remaining 5 families still run at Sharpe +1.26 — the robustness is in the decorrelation, not in that leg.

  • OOS is most of the history, not 2 years: the book-level walk-forward runs out-of-sample 2013→2026 (~13y) at Sharpe +3.86, paying for that history in drawdown (−10.5% vs −9.4%).
  • Equal weight is evidence-based: re-fitting the weights does not beat it out-of-sample: the mean-variance fit buys Sharpe (+4.63 vs +3.86) with a 1× deeper drawdown (−15% vs −11%).

Validation (§10) — Monte-Carlo, block-bootstrap P5/50/95: Sharpe +3.31/+3.96/+4.62 · max-DD −16.2%/−11.2%/−8.7% · monthly hit-rate 74%/80%/84%. Sharpe and drawdown clear their targets even on the 5th-percentile draw; months-in-profit does not (74% against ≥80%), so that is the target that depends on a kind path rather than on the edge. Mean cross-family correlation +0.06; remove the strongest leg (vol-prem) and the book still runs at Sharpe +1.03.

Construction (Task §2): 4 strategy families, each frozen and validated in its own deep-dive, are re-scaled to a common ~15% vol on a trailing (point-in-time, lagged) estimate and equal-weighted — risk parity with no performance-based selection, each family run uniformly across its whole universe (no per-asset mining), fixed seeds, no held-out block tuned against. The reported window is 2012–2026 and families contribute where live: 2012 runs on vol-prem and trend, x-sect and breakout join in 2020. On the fully cross-asset window (2020+) the book is Sharpe 3.21, CAGR +34.8%.

Honest reading of the headline: the 3.94 book Sharpe is dominated by the short-vol / variance-risk-premium leg — 82% of the book’s P&L — whose own Sharpe is high but sits on a real systemic-vol tail. That is why it is held at the same 1/4 risk weight as every other leg and never sized on its Sharpe. Remove it entirely and the remaining 3 families still run at Sharpe +1.03 — the robustness is in the decorrelation, not in that leg.

Construction (Task §2): eight strategy families, each frozen and validated in its own deep-dive, are re-scaled to a common ~15% vol on a trailing (point-in-time, lagged) estimate and equal-weighted — risk parity with no performance-based selection. The reported window is {report_window}; families contribute where live, so 2011–2019 runs on trend / vol-premium / cross-sectional and the crypto-perp legs (carry, breakout) join in 2020. On the fully cross-asset window (2020+) the book is Sharpe {ca_sharpe}, CAGR {ca_cagr}.

Honest reading of the headline: the {sharpe_ann} book Sharpe is dominated by the short-vol / variance-risk-premium leg, whose standalone Sharpe is high but sits on a real systemic-vol tail — remove it and the book runs at Sharpe {top_removed}. That is the point of the construction: at mean cross-family correlation {mean_corr} the families barely overlap, so the book earns its robustness from diversification, not from any single leg or any fitted selection. Each family runs uniformly across its whole universe (no per-asset mining); fixed seeds; no held-out block tuned against.
